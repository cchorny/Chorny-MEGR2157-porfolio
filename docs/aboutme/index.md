#About Me
  I'm a Mechanical Engineering student at UNC Charlotte, originally from Syracuse, New York. Growing up, sports were a major part of my life. I captained both my high school baseball and football teams and was fortunate to win several championships along the way.
  Becoming an engineer was a goal of mine coming out of high school, but my career initially took me in a different direction. I eventually began working at a Trane HVAC manufacturing facility, where I had opportunities to work alongside the OPEX (Operational Excellence) team on projects focused on improving production-line workflow and operational procedures.
  Those experiences renewed my interest in design, problem-solving, and finding practical ways to improve how systems operate. However, advancing further into that type of work required an advanced degree. That realization ultimately led me back to my original goal and to UNC Charlotte, where I am now pursuing my degree in Mechanical Engineering.
