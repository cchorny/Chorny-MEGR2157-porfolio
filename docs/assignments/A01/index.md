### [Aiden Bradley's Engineering Portfolio](https://aocb.github.io/)

Aiden Bradley's portfolio was well designed and it was clear that a significant amount of effort went into making the site visually appealing and presentable. The overall layout was organized and made it relatively easy to navigate between the different sections of the portfolio. However, I felt that the landing page and About Me section lacked some of the professionalism I would expect from an engineering portfolio. I also found that while some of the links worked as intended, others did not, which took away from the overall usability and polish of the site.

### [Sean Ware's Engineering Portfolio](https://seanware5.github.io/seanware/)

Sean Ware's portfolio had a very professional appearance and was clear and easy to navigate. One of its strongest features was the amount of documentation included with his projects. The additional information made it easy to understand the projects and the engineering work involved in them. The main weakness I noticed was that the portfolio lacked content overall. While the projects and information that were included were presented very well, adding more content would make the portfolio feel more complete.
